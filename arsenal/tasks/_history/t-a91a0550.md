Context compaction summarises a long task lossily: exact decisions, dead ends and the
next command are what a summary drops first. `execution` already keeps
`tmp/<task-id>-notes.md`, but with a free-form shape nothing reads back.

Done means: the notes template opens with a fixed resume block — **Decided** (do not
reopen), **Ruled out**, **Next step** (one command) — and `execution` says to refresh it
at milestones (after RED, after GREEN, after a decision), not every turn. Changed files
are deliberately not listed: `git status` is the truth and a hand-kept list goes stale.
