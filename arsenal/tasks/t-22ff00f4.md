**Severity:** medium · **Confidence:** high · **Category:** security

**Where:** `plugins/core/skills/init/assets/bin/park_task.sh:31,52, plugins/core/skills/init/assets/bin/check_update.sh (REMOTE=${ARSENAL_REMOTE:-arsenal})`

### Finding
Every other queue script uses ARSENAL_QUEUE_REMOTE (default origin); park_task.sh reads ARSENAL_REMOTE, which check_update.sh defines as the upstream marketplace remote (default name 'arsenal'). A consumer who exports ARSENAL_REMOTE for update checks gets their whole working tree (git add -A, untracked files included) force-pushed to arsenal/wip/<id> on the marketplace remote instead of their own.

### Evidence
grep of the two scripts: park_task `REMOTE="${ARSENAL_REMOTE:-origin}"`, check_update `REMOTE="${ARSENAL_REMOTE:-arsenal}"`, claim_task/open_task_pr/rebase_stack use ARSENAL_QUEUE_REMOTE.

### Suggested direction
Use ARSENAL_QUEUE_REMOTE in park_task.sh.
