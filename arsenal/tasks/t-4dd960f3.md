### Finding
`execution` defaults to test-first for every change and gates reward that a test exists, never that it earns its runtime; nothing ever deletes a test. Measured test:source line ratios in repos built with the workflow: ai-chat-exporter 25k:18k, claude-arsenal shell 17k:6k, integral-job-search 82k:105k, drawspec 34k:76k plus goldens. The owner reports slow CI across projects.

### Evidence
Line counts via `git ls-files | xargs wc -l` split by test/non-test path on 2026-11-11; owner report of slow CI.

### Suggested direction
Add a risk-based 'is this test owed' criterion to execution/review, ship tiered + path-filtered CI in the /init workflow templates with a fast-tier time budget, and add a periodic prune step driven by mutmut-report/pin-check.
